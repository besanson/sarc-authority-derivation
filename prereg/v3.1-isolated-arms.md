## Provenance of the correction

Direct inspection of `experiments.py`'s current `run_seed_workflow`
(v0.1 through v0.3, unchanged by any of those revisions) finds a real
state-sharing defect, independent of and not raised by the external
review that motivated v3: the three policy arms this artifact compares
-- baseline (real `sarc_governance` evaluation of the imported
`authority.yaml`), derived (admits iff the v2 loss registry says safe),
and over-inclusive (derived, plus a spurious escalation whenever
`workflow == "W2"`) -- are evaluated inside **one** decision loop against
**one** shared `budget_remaining` float and **one** shared `GrantLedger`
instance:

- `budget_remaining` is decremented once per decision by
  `if baseline_admits or derived_admits: budget_remaining -= order_value_post`
  (`experiments.py`, `run_seed_workflow`) -- baseline's own admission can
  deplete the pool that derived's *later* verdict then reads (via
  `StateTuple.budget_remaining`, which `spend_against_depleted_delegated_
  budget` reads directly), and vice versa. Neither arm's budget
  trajectory reflects *only its own* admission history.
- `ledger = GrantLedger()` is a single instance: `ledger.issue`/`ledger.
  consume` are called once per decision regardless of which arm's
  admission is being evaluated, and the resulting `consumed_grant_ids()`
  is read into the one `StateTuple` every arm's verdict is computed from
  (`replayed_consumed_grant` reads exactly this field). A grant "consumed"
  on one arm's notional behalf is consumed for all three.
- The over-inclusive arm has no admission or state trajectory of its own
  at all today: it is not a policy that admits or denies anything, only a
  counter (`spurious_escalations_overinclusive`) layered on top of
  derived's own admit/deny decision. It cannot currently diverge from
  derived's own budget/ledger consumption even in principle.

Because `true_violations`, `baseline_missed`, and `spurious_escalations_
overinclusive` are all read from the *same* per-decision `state` (which
embeds the shared, cross-contaminated `budget_remaining` and `consumed_
grant_ids`), every CH-A1/CH-A3/CH-A4 magnitude v0.1-v0.3 report is
measured on a decision stream where one arm's hypothetical admission can
silently change another arm's inputs. This is a real threat to the
sweep's internal validity, not a cosmetic naming issue (contrast v3's
core-vs-reduct correction, which corrected a *proof claim*; this
corrects the *experiment's own plumbing*) -- registered here, before any
refactor, so the fix is checked against a stated hypothesis rather than
silently applied.

## Definitions

**Arm.** One of `{baseline, derived, over_inclusive}` -- a policy
function from (state-so-far, current decision) to an admit/deny verdict,
evaluated against the *same* underlying decision stream (`day`, `sku`,
`proposed_qty`, `read_cost`, `true_cost`, `actor_role`, `resource_class`,
`frozen`, `is_replay_probe` -- environmental facts about the world at
decision time, common to all three arms by design, since the whole point
of a controlled comparison is holding the environment fixed) as every
other arm.

**ArmState (v3.1, new).** Per arm, per (seed, workflow) run: `{budget_
remaining: float, ledger: GrantLedger, admission_history: List[bool]}`.
Three `ArmState` instances are constructed per run, one per arm.

**Isolation invariant (what v3.1 registers, to be enforced by the
contamination regression test, Phase B3).** For each arm `A` and each
decision `d`: `A`'s `ArmState` is read to compute `A`'s own verdict on
`d`, and is mutated *only* by `A`'s own verdict on `d` -- never by
another arm's verdict, and never by a second, differently-derived
verdict for the same arm. Concretely: `A.budget_remaining` decreases by
`d.order_value_post` iff `A` itself admits `d` (not "iff `baseline` or
`derived` admits"); `A.ledger.consume(...)` is called with `A`'s own
admission outcome, so `A.ledger.consumed_grant_ids()` reflects only
grants `A` itself has admitted.

**Initialization (unchanged across arms).** All three `ArmState`s start
identically per (seed, workflow): the same `period_budget` (Definition
unchanged, `calibrate_period_budget`), an empty `GrantLedger`, empty
history. Divergence across arms is then a pure consequence of their
differing admission decisions applied to identical initial conditions,
never a difference in starting state.

**Decision rules (unchanged from v0.1-v0.3; only the state each rule
reads is now arm-local, not shared):**

- **baseline**: `op_composition.evaluate_sarc_pag(...)` against the
  imported `authority.yaml` and the role/value-cap arguments -- reads no
  `ArmState` field at all today (the imported baseline has no budget or
  grant concept of its own) and is therefore unaffected by isolation
  except that it now maintains its *own* (unused) `ArmState` for
  symmetry and future extension.
- **derived**: `not m_verdict(state, registry)` where `state.budget_
  remaining` and `state.consumed_grant_ids` are now `derived`'s *own*
  `ArmState` fields, not the shared ones.
- **over_inclusive**: `derived`'s own rule (evaluated against
  `over_inclusive`'s *own* `ArmState`, which may consequently diverge
  from `derived`'s the moment their admission histories first differ) AND
  an additional forced denial whenever `workflow == "W2"` -- this is the
  measured cost of treating the one non-participating (v1) / redundant
  (v2) candidate property as authority-bearing, restated as its own
  policy with its own trajectory rather than a counter bolted onto
  another arm's.

## Hypothesis (registered two-sided; either outcome is a result)

**v3.1 isolation hypothesis.** Re-measuring all 30 registered seeds
(`prereg/seeds.json`) across both workflows with isolated `ArmState`s
(v0.4) instead of the shared state above (v0.3, frozen) may or may not
materially change any CH-A1/CH-A3/CH-A4 magnitude or SUPPORTED/NOT
SUPPORTED status. **Material movement**, decided mechanically, not by
eyeballing: for each of `ch_a1_baseline_missed`, `ch_a1_true_violations`,
`ch_a3_off_admissions`, `ch_a3_replay_probe_count`, `ch_a3_escalation_
overhead`, and `spurious_escalations_overinclusive`, v0.4's re-measured
mean falls outside v0.3's own committed 95% CI; OR any of `ch_a1_derived_
zero_every_cell`, `ch_a3_on_zero_every_cell`, `ch_a3_off_nonzero_at_
least_one_cell`, `ch_a1_status`, `ch_a2_status`, `ch_a3_status`, `ch_a4_
status` flips value between v0.3 and v0.4. **SUPPORTED** (isolation
mattered): at least one of the above holds, reported with the specific
metric(s) and both values, not smoothed over. **NOT SUPPORTED**
(isolation was contamination without consequence for any decision-
relevant quantity, given this artifact's own declared parameters): none
of the above holds. Neither outcome is assumed; v0.3's own numbers are
not retroactively treated as wrong regardless of which outcome obtains
-- they are what v0.3's own (contaminated) design actually measured, and
stay committed as such.

**Contamination regression test (registered here, written in Phase B3):**
a synthetic two-decision case where decision 1 is baseline-ADMIT and
derived-BLOCK, asserting that only baseline's own `ArmState.budget_
remaining` changes after decision 1 -- derived's and over_inclusive's
must be exactly their initialized value. This is the direct, minimal,
mechanical witness of the isolation invariant above, independent of and
prior to the 30-seed re-measurement.

## Registration discipline

- No `experiments.py` edit, no `ArmState` class, no contamination
  regression test, and no v0.4 sweep re-run may happen before this file
  is committed and tagged `prereg-p5-v3.1`, by the author, via the GitHub
  web interface.
- v0.1/v0.2/v0.3's own results, checkers, and outputs stay exactly as
  committed -- byte-frozen apart from the informational `generated_at_
  head_sha` stamp any fresh `make formal` run naturally refreshes.
- The decision rules themselves (what each arm admits, given its own
  state) are unchanged by v3.1 -- only which state each rule reads
  changes, from shared to arm-local. A different decision rule is out of
  scope for this correction.
- Whatever CH-A1/CH-A3/CH-A4 v0.4 reports, `NOVELTY.md` and the v0.4
  draft state the isolation hypothesis's actual outcome (SUPPORTED or
  NOT SUPPORTED) plainly, the same discipline v2's and v3's own
  amendments already follow.
