arXiv submission metadata
==========================

Title (verbatim from the paper)
--------------------------------
Minimal Sufficient Governance Context: Compiling Loss Models and Reachable Execution Semantics into Cost-Aware Runtime Authority Contracts for Autonomous Agents

Author line
-----------
Gaston Besanson (Universidad Torcuato Di Tella)

Category suggestion
--------------------
Primary: cs.AI (Artificial Intelligence)
  Rationale: the paper's central contribution is a machine-checked
  derivation and synthesis procedure -- from a declared loss model and an
  executable-reachable action set, compile the minimal, cost-aware
  runtime observation sets sufficient to make every modeled safety
  distinction, with certificates and counterexamples a downstream system
  consumes directly. This is squarely an AI-systems formal-methods
  contribution to autonomous-agent authority derivation.

Cross-list: cs.LO (Logic in Computer Science)
  Rationale: the derivation is built on discernibility sets (Skowron and
  Rauszer 1992) restated as a hitting-set/CNF problem, solved via SAT and
  MaxSAT encodings, with machine-checked soundness, minimality, and
  sufficiency certificates.

Cross-list: cs.CR (Cryptography and Security)
  Rationale: the paper's subject is authority/access-control gate design
  for autonomous systems -- minimal sufficient observation sets for a
  pre-action authority gate, contrasted against declared-only manual
  policies, ABAC mining, and essential-variable analysis baselines.
